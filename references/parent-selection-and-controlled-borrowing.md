# Parent Selection And Controlled Borrowing

Read this reference after the format specification is frozen and before choosing or editing a CSL.

## Search reproducibly

Record the official styles source, snapshot identity or commit, retrieval date, and license. Check exact journal identity first using title variants, abbreviations, ISSN/eISSN, self links, and dependent-style relationships. If no exact match exists, create a shortlist from local structural and behavioral signals and inspect only the serious candidates.

Automatic scoring may form a shortlist, but it must not choose the parent. Apply candidates lexicographically:

1. non-compensable hard requirements;
2. citation and bibliography behavior differences;
3. edit surface, macro dependencies, provenance, license, and maintenance risk.

A high aggregate score cannot compensate for violating a frozen hard rule.

## Choose one structural owner

The structural parent owns the root options, metadata history, main macro graph, citation and bibliography layouts, sorting/disambiguation policy, and default locale/term behavior. Copy it to an independent working file, assign a unique ID/title/self link, record the template relationship when appropriate, and never edit the downloaded original.

An existing independent CSL may serve as the working baseline for an audit even when it has no parent.

## Borrow components by dependency closure

Borrowing from a donor style is allowed when it lowers risk. Before transplanting a component, record the donor identity and license, intended behavior, required macros/terms/options, collisions and delimiter ownership, effects on global behavior, and baseline/post-transplant regression results.

Transplant the smallest coherent dependency closure, then adapt it to the structural parent's conventions. Do not paste isolated XML whose required macros or global settings are unknown.

Treat the work as unsupported composite/from-scratch construction and stop if it requires competing macro systems, replacement of multiple major control surfaces, or broad architecture redesign. Report what each candidate contributes, why a single-parent adaptation is insufficient, and what additional validation would be required. Do not continue merely because the user repeats the delivery request; route continuation to a separately designed experimental workflow or a substantive Skill update with its own contract and quality gate.

## Preserve provenance

Retain required attribution and license information. Do not imply that copied or adapted logic was authored from nothing. If license compatibility or redistribution rights are unclear, stop before copying and obtain authoritative license evidence.
